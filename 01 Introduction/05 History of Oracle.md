# History of Oracle

## 1. What is Oracle?

**Oracle** is a database system used to store and manage a large volume of facts and figures.

To understand Oracle today, let's look at how the company and its name changed over time.

---

## 2. History of Oracle — Step by Step

### 1977

In **1977**, **Larry Ellison**, **Bob Miner**, and **Ed Oates** cofounded Oracle Corporation.

At that time, the company was called:

> **Software Development Laboratory (SDL)**

```text
1977
   ↓
Software Development Laboratory
   ↓
Larry Ellison + Bob Miner + Ed Oates
```

---

### 1979

In **1979**, SDL was renamed to:

> **Relational Software Corporation (RSI)**

```text
1977 → SDL
          ↓
1979 → RSI
```

---

### 1982

In **1982**, RSI was renamed to:

> **Oracle System Corporation (OSC)**

```text
1977 → SDL
1979 → RSI
1982 → OSC
```

---

### 1995

In **1995**, OSC was renamed to:

> **Oracle Corporation**

```text
1982 → Oracle System Corporation
             ↓
1995 → Oracle Corporation
```

Your material then states that officially the company is called:

> **Oracle**
