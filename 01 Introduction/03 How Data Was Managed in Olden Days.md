# How Data Was Managed in Olden Days

## 1. What was used?

In olden days, **data was managed using a ledger**.

A **ledger** is a book/register in which information was written and maintained manually.

For example:

```text
Student Details

Name       : Basha
Age        : 23
Gender     : Male
Branch     : ECE
Student ID : 458
Percentage : 93
```

---

## 2. Simple Example

Imagine a college maintaining student information.

The information could be written in a physical ledger:

```text
+------+--------+-----+--------+
| ID   | Name   | Age | Marks  |
+------+--------+-----+--------+
| 458  | Basha  | 23  | 93     |
| 459  | Ravi   | 22  | 88     |
| 460  | John   | 24  | 91     |
+------+--------+-----+--------+
```

The information was maintained manually in such records.

---

## 3. What was the Problem?

When the amount of information was **small**, maintaining it in a ledger was manageable.

But when the amount of information became **large**, managing the facts and figures became difficult.

For example:

```text
100 students       → manageable
10,000 students    → difficult
1,00,000 students  → very difficult
```

Finding, updating, and maintaining a particular piece of information would become difficult.

---

## 4. Why did a better system become necessary?

The main requirements were:

```text
Facts & Figures
      ↓
Store Permanently
      +
Retrieve Efficiently
```

Manual ledgers made managing a large volume of information difficult.

This eventually led to the need for computerized methods of managing data, and your material next introduces programming languages and then DBMS. 

---

## 5. Important Point

Remember the sequence:

```text
OLDEN DAYS
     ↓
   LEDGER
     ↓
Manual management of data
     ↓
Large volume became difficult
     ↓
Programming Languages
     ↓
DBMS
```
